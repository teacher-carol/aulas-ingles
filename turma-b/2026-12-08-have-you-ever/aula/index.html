Grammar

have / has + past participle

I have been to Recife.

She has eaten sushi.

I have never flown.

Have you ever tried tapioca?

✅ Yes, I have. · ❌ No, I haven't. / No, never.

We use it for experiences in your life, when the time is not important.

Exercise 1

Choose the participle

Have you ever ___ a famous person? (meet)

The big rule

❌ WRONG

I have been to Recife last year.

✅ CORRECT

I went to Recife last year.

Finished time ➜ past simple

Have you ever been to Recife? ➜ Yes, I have! ➜ When did you go? ➜ I went in 2023.

Speaking · 10 min

Have you ever...? 🎤

Ask me, then I ask you. If the answer is YES, ask a follow-up in the past: When? Where? How was it?

...been on a plane?

...stayed up all night?

...met a famous person?

...lost something important?

...cooked for a lot of people?

...ridden a horse?

Game · 5 min

True or false? 🕵️

Say 3 sentences with I have... or I have never.... One is false. Can I guess?

I have never eaten pizza. I have been to 5 states. I have ridden a camel.

Extra time? ⭐ Bonus

The fingers game ✋

We both start with 5 fingers up. Say: I have never... If the other person HAS done it, they put one finger down and say when.

➜ I have! I ate it in 2023. (one finger down)
