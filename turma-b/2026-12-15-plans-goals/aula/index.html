Grammar

going to or will?

going to = a plan you already decided

will = a decision NOW, a prediction or a promise

📖 Read

I'm going to visit my grandparents in January.

I think it will be a great year.

Form

❌ WRONG

I going to travel.

I will to study.

She wills help.

✅ CORRECT

I'm going to travel.

I will study.

She will help.

Be careful with the form!

Negative: I'm not going to... · I won't...
