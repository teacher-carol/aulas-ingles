Grammar · Reported speech

Tell the gossip: one step back in time ⏪

She said: "..."She said that...
I am pregnant.she was pregnant.
I live with my boyfriend.she lived with her boyfriend.
I can't come.she couldn't come.
I will tell Mom.she would tell Mom.
I'm going to quit.she was going to quit.

💡 The pronouns change too: I ➜ she / he · my ➜ her / his · that is optional
